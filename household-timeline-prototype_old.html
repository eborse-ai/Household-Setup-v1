AI Suggestions
Control how AI-predicted future events and goal gaps are displayed on the timeline. The underlying predictive model is managed by the Agentforce team.
ℹ️ Settings here control display behavior only — the underlying prediction model is owned and managed by the Agentforce platform.
Show AI Suggested toggle in timeline header
When enabled, advisors can show/hide AI suggestions using the toggle. When disabled, all AI suggestions are always visible.
Default state: AI suggestions visible
When toggled on, AI suggestions are shown by default when an advisor opens the timeline.
Show Accept / Reject actions on AI suggestions
When accepted, Agentforce automatically creates the corresponding record (e.g., Goal, Life Event) with pre-filled data.
Require advisor reason when rejecting a suggestion
Rejection reason is fed back to the model for continuous improvement.
